01 / The conference

Where science
meets business.

CMBEBIH is an international conference bringing together researchers, engineers, professionals, industry leaders and innovators to drive interdisciplinary innovation and sustainable development.

Guided by the motto From science to business, the conference connects scientific excellence with technological innovation, entrepreneurship and real-world impact.

Explore past editions ↗

CMBEBIH’s 12-year journey reflects its ongoing commitment to advancing science, fostering collaboration and providing a world-class platform for innovation. With each edition, the conference continues to grow in influence and impact.

Read the welcome message (PDF) ↗
“Bridging science, technology and society — one discovery at a time.”

CMBEBIH is the leading international conference on Medical and Biological Engineering in Southeast Europe. Since 2015, it has served as an essential meeting point where cutting-edge research meets real-world healthcare needs.

Organised by the Bosnia and Herzegovina Medical and Biological Engineering Society, every edition brings together scientists, clinicians, engineers, entrepreneurs and policymakers — united by a single goal: turning innovation into impact.

From biosensors to AI-driven diagnostics, from tissue engineering to sustainable healthcare systems, CMBEBIH covers the full spectrum of biomedical engineering — always with one eye on what it means for patients and societies.

OUR MISSION

Connecting knowledge with opportunity.

To foster scientific excellence, interdisciplinary collaboration and knowledge exchange while strengthening connections between research, industry and government.

OUR VISION

An innovation ecosystem without borders.

To contribute to an internationally connected research and innovation ecosystem where people, knowledge and infrastructures are accessible, and ideas can become real-world solutions.

OUR STORY · ESTABLISHED 2015

What began as a meeting point for medical and biological engineering has grown into an interdisciplinary platform connecting research communities across health, artificial intelligence, biotechnology, environment, agrifood and emerging technologies.

03 / For authors

Bring your
research forward.

Everything you need to prepare and submit your research, including author guidelines, manuscript templates, publication opportunities and submission instructions.

Call for papers ↗
A

Manuscript preparation

Manuscripts must be written in English and prepared using the official Springer Nature Conference Proceedings templates (Microsoft Word or LaTeX). Full papers should be 6–10 pages, including figures, tables and references.

Each submission must include a title, author information and affiliations, an abstract (maximum 250 words) and keywords. Submissions must present original work and comply with Springer Nature's ethical and editorial policies.

B

Submission & review

Submit electronically through Springer Nature Meteor. Select the relevant conference track, enter manuscript details and upload your files. Submissions undergo peer review; accepted authors will receive instructions for final manuscripts and publishing agreements.

C

Important dates

Paper submission deadline21 March 2027

Notification of acceptance30 April 2027

Revised paper deadline23 May 2027
