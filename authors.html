Call for papers ↗
A

Manuscript preparation

Manuscripts must be written in English and prepared using the official Springer Nature Conference Proceedings templates (Microsoft Word or LaTeX). Full papers should be 6–10 pages, including figures, tables and references.

Each submission must include a title, author information and affiliations, an abstract (maximum 250 words) and keywords. Submissions must present original work and comply with Springer Nature's ethical and editorial policies.

B

Submission & review

Submit electronically through Springer Nature Meteor. Select the relevant conference track, enter manuscript details and upload your files. Submissions undergo peer review; accepted authors will receive instructions for final manuscripts and publishing agreements.
