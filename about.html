01 / The conference

Where science
meets NEXT business?

CMBEBIH is an international conference bringing together researchers, engineers, professionals, industry leaders and innovators to drive interdisciplinary innovation and sustainable development.

Guided by the motto From science to business, the conference connects scientific excellence with technological innovation, entrepreneurship and real-world impact.

Explore past editions ↗

CMBEBIH’s 12-year journey reflects its ongoing commitment to advancing science, fostering collaboration and providing a world-class platform for innovation. With each edition, the conference continues to grow in influence and impact.

Read the welcome message (PDF) ↗
OUR MISSION

Connecting knowledge with opportunity.

To foster scientific excellence, interdisciplinary collaboration and knowledge exchange while strengthening connections between research, industry and government.

OUR VISION

An innovation ecosystem without borders.

To contribute to an internationally connected research and innovation ecosystem where people, knowledge and infrastructures are accessible, and ideas can become real-world solutions.

OUR STORY · ESTABLISHED 2015

What began as a meeting point for medical and biological engineering has grown into an interdisciplinary platform connecting research communities across health, artificial intelligence, biotechnology, environment, agrifood and emerging technologies.
